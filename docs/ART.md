# 3D assets

The first-person game uses authored or redistributable local 3D assets with physically based materials. Asset provenance, paths and licenses are recorded in ASSETS.md and the runtime asset manifest as assets are integrated.

The old image-based scenery in public/scenes/ and docs/art-source/ is retained during the rebuild as historical source material. Its provenance is preserved in [archive/ART.md](archive/ART.md); it is not a replacement for navigable terrain or finished 3D models.
